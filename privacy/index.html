Privacy Policy / Chính sách quyền riêng tư

Video Studio · Last updated: 2026-11-18

1. What we access from TikTok

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar — only to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload / video.publish: to send the videos you choose to your TikTok drafts or profile.

2. Where data is stored

Video Studio runs on your own computer. Access tokens, your media and generated videos are stored locally on that computer. There is no central server that collects your data. This website only hosts these pages and forwards the TikTok login result back to the app on your own computer; it stores nothing.

Mọi dữ liệu lưu trên máy của bạn; trang web này không lưu gì.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Media is sent only to TikTok when you start an upload, and to AI providers you configure yourself.

4. Deleting your data

Disconnect the TikTok account in the app to delete its tokens, delete posts/videos in the app, or revoke access in TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
